Flutter Web · scrolling semanticsHuman-approved · not merged

Making semantic hit targets match the rows you see.

Problem. After a scrollable list shrank, browser semantics offsets could stay stale: selecting the visually painted Row 4 activated Row 2 instead.

Approach. Reset stale DOM scroll adjustments when a node stops scrolling, then recheck semantic positions and hit testing.

Independent review. Flutter reviewer flutter-zl verified the change with before/after Web demos and approved PR #195578 on 3 November 2026. It remains open.
